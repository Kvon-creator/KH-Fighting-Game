Common Pipeline Pitfalls

Floaty Passing Poses: If the foot does not drop quickly enough between the Up phase (Drawing 4) and the Contact phase (Drawing 1), the run will look like low-gravity moonwalking. The Z-axis drop curve must be aggressively skewed so the foot slams down into the contact frame.
