# Round 52 - Upper Slash concept (crouching Heavy), prompt UPPER_SLASH_CONCEPT_ROUND_51.txt

Kvon, November 6: the crouching Heavy should be KH2's Upper Slash. His GIF (`design/references/Upper Slash.gif`) was
too unclear to read the poses from, and the KH Wiki has no description of the motion, only the mechanics (ground
combo finisher that knocks the target into the air). So we asked Gemini for a pose sheet, attaching only Sora's own
renders (kneel crouch_hold2d_v18 and battle idle sprite2d_v3).

Result: `gemini_original_preview.jpg` (1024x687 RGB JPEG; the original download is beside it). 6 poses.
- Good: our style, outfit and Key. Pose 4's big upward circular smear in front (low -> overhead) reads as an upper
  slash and fits the GIF's rough rising swing. Poses 4-5 finish two-handed, with the Key raised overhead.
- Problems: pose 4 (the hit) is AIRBORNE (both feet off the floor), but Kvon wants him grounded. Poses 2-3 already
  hold the Key up behind him, so the low-to-high swing in front only exists in the smear. The grip changes from one
  hand (1-3) to two (4-6). Pose 1 is not the attached kneel.
- Status: concept only, pending Kvon. Not used for any game art.

## Round 54 (fresh chat, UPPER_SLASH_CONCEPT_ROUND_54.txt, both references attached)
`round_54/gemini_original_preview.jpg` (1024x765). It meets the three corrections: grounded, one hand (right), and the
Key travels from low to high: 1 kneel with the Key low behind; 2 Key forward-down in front, tip near the floor;
3 rising, Key up past him with a smear from the floor in front; 4 standing tall on the floor, Key overhead pointing back,
big upward smear in front; 5 follow-through overhead; 6 back to the crouch.
Differences from what the game needs: in poses 4-5 the Key is already past the top (behind his head), and the hitbox
should be in front, so the 3D active drawing should be the Key sweeping up in front at head height, with Gemini's
pose 4 as the follow-through. In 4-5 his chest faces the camera; keep the idle's 3/4 facing. Pending Kvon.
