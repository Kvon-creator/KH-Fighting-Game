## Review of attempt05

The smooth cel shading, dark linework, face/hair, KH2 costume, red cloth, white trim, yellow straps and boot finish match the approved idle closely. The pose faces right in three-quarter view. The near hand holds the connected black grip inside the gold guard with a plausible wrist orientation; the opposite hand is free. The blue collar, shaft and silver head connect in the reviewed same-arm shoulder-carry direction. The shaft has a visible front face compatible with the requested slight foreground perspective; this is a qualitative image assessment, not a measured 3D angle.

The earlier requirement for exclusively rectangular notches was a reviewer mistake: it elevated the simplified construction master above the approved finished idle. No change to the user's Kingdom Key design was requested. Rounded cutouts alone are not a reason to reject this pilot. Keep the approved finished design consistent across future frames.

This is single-frame artwork approval by Codex only. It does not establish constant weapon length across a cycle, alternating gait, continuity, timing, start/stop seams or final user approval. The revised copies are not independent animation drawings.

Transparent export, consistent 512px canvas/root registration and 128px game-scale review still belong to the later sprite-production step. Temporary scaled inspection previews are not claimed as new animation frames.

Approved idle, reviewed run frame, supplied carry photograph, weapon master and existing local animation candidates are unchanged. No production art or engine file was replaced. Final user approval is pending.
