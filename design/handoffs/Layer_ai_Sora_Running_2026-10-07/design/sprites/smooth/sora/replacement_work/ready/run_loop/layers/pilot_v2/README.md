# Foreground carry pilot

Open Foreground_Comparison.png to compare the previous flat carry with a tip yawed 25 degrees toward the viewer. This is a subtle perspective construction preview, not an approved finished sprite or animation.

The same unchanged weapon master is projected through a single camera transform. Physical master-space length stays 285 pixels; apparent lengths and widths change together through perspective. Grip stays at (405,225). The complete guard, handle, collar, shaft and teeth share the projection. manifest.json records the camera, anchors and transformation. Source sheets and pilot_v1 remain intact.

Torso reconstruction and forearm are still rough. Wrist depth, shoulder contact, coherent body rotation and finger wrap require further local refinement before propagating to other frames. This pilot only demonstrates weapon perspective; it does not claim coordinated animated body movement. The eventual gait should vary modest depth orientation in step with actual shoulder/torso poses rather than applying a static pasted weapon or distorting body frames.
