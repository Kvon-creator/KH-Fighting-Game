# Sora Crouching Light

**Fact or rule:** Sora's crouching Light attack is a quick low Kingdom Key slash.

**Why:** K'von selected this motion during basic-attack design review. No further reason was provided.

**Start, October 27:** Kvon: "Yes" (do the Crouching Light). Gemini image mode still would not turn on (tools menu does not open), so I used an online reference as for the Heavy: Dustloop, Guilty Gear Strive Ky Kiske 2S ("a low-hitting counterpart to f.S", 11 startup / 2 active / 24 recovery), image saved as `design/references/ky_ggst_2s_reference.png` - a low crouching sweep with a flat floor-level elliptical smear; better suited to the Crouching Medium (wide low sweep). For the Light: Ky's low forward-leaning posture with a short quick one-handed forehand slash from the approved crouch (crouch_hold_v4: right hand low behind, left fist at the chest) ending at shin height in front, back to the crouch. Built as `atk_clight_vN`; frame data proposal 5/3/12.
