# Sora Crouching Light

**Fact or rule:** Sora's crouching Light attack is a quick low Kingdom Key slash.

**Why:** K'von selected this motion during basic-attack design review. No further reason was provided.

**Start, October 27:** Kvon: "Yes" (do the Crouching Light). Gemini image mode still would not turn on (tools menu does not open), so I used an online reference as for the Heavy: Dustloop, Guilty Gear Strive Ky Kiske 2S ("a low-hitting counterpart to f.S", 11 startup / 2 active / 24 recovery), image saved as `design/references/ky_ggst_2s_reference.png` - a low crouching sweep with a flat floor-level elliptical smear; better suited to the Crouching Medium (wide low sweep). For the Light: Ky's low forward-leaning posture with a short quick one-handed forehand slash from the approved crouch (crouch_hold_v4: right hand low behind, left fist at the chest) ending at shin height in front, back to the crouch. Built as `atk_clight_vN`; frame data proposal 5/3/12.

**MoMask trial, October28:** Kvon: "Start with the MoMask demo to create a refined version of the crouching light attack" (v8 still unreviewed). Free public Hugging Face Space MeYourHint/MoMask (Gradio 3.24.1, fn_index 3: prompt, seconds, "IK"; 20 fps BVH, 22-joint skeleton, always starts standing, fixed seed; all users share `./cached/12138/` so check each download visually). Three prompts tried (a1 kneel-sword, a2 crouch-slash, a3 crouch pull-back-and-swipe); none kneels. a3 is closest: right arm high then swiped down/forward, ending in a deep crouch with the arm out low. MoMask gives body mechanics only (no weapon, no kneel), so use it as an upper-body/timing reference on top of the v18 kneel, not a replacement. Files (BVH, MP4, stick-figure sheets, prompts, and the client/sheet scripts) are in `design/models/sora/momask_trial/`.

**MoMask-refined builds, October28 (Kvon: "Continue with the MoMask method"; pending review):** `momask_trial/scripts/analyze_swing.py` measured a3 per frame (`a3/swing_analysis.json`): wind-up f10-16 hand up/back past the shoulder, swipe f17-21 (~4 m/s) with hips dropping .25 m, follow-through f22-28 torso pitch 13 -> 58 and right shoulder leading +16 -> +25. Applied, scaled to the kneel and a Light, as `atk_clight_v9` (high wind-up with the blade angled toward the camera, hips drop .06, pitch 24/32 at hit/follow, twist -6/6/12/16/6; same kneel legs, drawings and 5/3/12 timing). v9's draw->sweep smear covered his face and the hit smear was an upright fin; v10 = no sweep smear, sweep blade nearly level; v11 = new opt-in `smear_bow: False` key (make_smears.py) so the hit smear follows the chop's real path (a downward wedge) instead of the low-swing upward bow. Review: `atk_clight2d_v11/Crouching_Light_compare.png` (v8 top, v11 bottom) + slow GIFs (`momask_trial/scripts/make_review.py`). 

**Correction, October28:** Kvon: "the poses need to be fixed. The range of the attack can stay the same, but Sora needs to turn his torso so that the arm is clipping through him as he attacks" (read as: so the arm is NOT clipping). Cause: v9-v11 turned his body toward the camera through the swing (turn -18/-22), so the right arm reached across his chest and the guard sat in his front thigh/shin. v12: hips stay at the crouch turn, upper-body `twist` toward the opponent (sweep 25, hit 40, follow 45, back1 20). v13: hands moved straight toward the camera (V_ is the orthographic view axis, so on-screen path and reach are unchanged) - sweep/hit +.14, follow +.16 - and sweep twist 35. v14 (current, pending review): first return drawing's hand +.12 toward the camera too. Rule: to pull a limb out of the body without changing the sprite, move it along V_. Review `atk_clight2d_v14/Crouching_Light_compare.png` (v11 top, v14 bottom). Clipping views: `momask_trial/scripts/clip_check.py`.
