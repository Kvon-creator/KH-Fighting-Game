# Input buffer (5 frames, everywhere)

**Fact or rule (Kvon, November 11):** "Go for the 5-frame buffer and apply it to all of the available inputs": attack recovery, landing, blockstun, hitstun and wake-up (plus the hit-freeze, which already had a one-frame version).

**Why:** Kvon asked after I explained attack buffering (comparable games: SF6 4 frames, Strive ~5); no further reason given.

**Built (fighter.gd):** INPUT_BUFFER 5, BUFFERED_KEYS up / light / medium / heavy / dash (the grab = Medium + Heavy). `_press_at[k]` = the game frame of the last new press, recorded every frame in every state (`_record_presses`, also during hit-freeze via `buffer_input`). A press counts while `_tick - press <= 5`: up to 5 frames before the first frame he can act. A move that uses a press clears it (`_use`) so it can't come out twice (e.g. a buffered jump becoming a double jump). Cleared when he is hit / grabbed / knocked down (`_end_moves`) and on reset. Directions (forward / back) are NOT buffered - double taps start glides and back-dashes. Replaced the old `_buffered` dict; presses during hitstun no longer get thrown away.
**Grab buffer (Kvon, November 11: "a bit longer"):** GRAB_BUFFER 8 - Medium + Heavy both pressed up to 8 frames before he can act still gives the grab (attacks / jump / dash stay at 5). Tested: 8 early yes, 9 early no.
**Tested:** recovery (Medium 3 frames before the Light's end -> first actionable frame; 8 early -> dropped), landing (Medium during a jL cut by the landing -> first ground frame), hitstun (5 early yes, 6 early no), wake-up (5 yes, 6 no), blockstun, double jump, L -> M chain, grab, CPU match - no errors.

**Attack buffer 8 frames (Kvon, November 17: "I was struggling to cancel S.H into C.H. Increase the input buffering window"):** fighter.gd ATTACK_BUFFER = 8 for Light / Medium / Heavy presses; jump and dash stay at INPUT_BUFFER 5 (my choice: a longer jump buffer causes unwanted jumps); the grab stays 8. Measured for sH -> cH (sH connects 13 frames after it starts): before, a Down + Heavy press counted only from 4 frames before contact (or after the hit); now from 6 frames before. Presses earlier in the swing still drop - the next step if Kvon still struggles: a bigger number, or remembering a cancel press made at any point during the attack until it connects (many fighting games do this). A real whiff still can't cancel; the chains and same-strength cancels are unchanged.

**Cancel presses remembered until contact (Kvon, November 17: chose option 2 after the 8-frame buffer helped only 2 frames):** fighter.gd `_pending` - a Light / Medium / Heavy press made while an attack is out (ground or air) is kept until that attack touches the opponent (hit or guard), then counts as a press at that moment, so the normal cancel rules decide (chains, stand <-> crouch once per strength, cL -> cL once, air chains). Forgotten when the attack ends without contact or is cut short (hit, grab, reset). The direction (Down for the crouching version) is read when the cancel comes out. Tested: sH -> cH from a press anywhere in the swing (was only the last 6 frames before contact); a real whiff doesn't cancel; sL > sM > cH pressed early; sL > cL > sL still blocked; sH + Heavy again without Down doesn't restart it; jM -> jH pressed 2 frames into the jM. Possible side effect to watch: mashing a button during an attack, then holding Down, cancels into the crouching version.
