# Sora Standing Heavy

**Fact or rule:** Sora's standing Heavy attack is an ADVANCING DOWNWARD SLASH: he steps forward with his back foot (it passes the front foot and becomes the new front foot) to increase his range while bringing the Kingdom Key down from overhead. (Replaced the wide spinning slash on October 27.)

**Why:** K'von selected this motion during basic-attack design review. No further reason was provided.

**Concept, October 27:** Kvon: "Move on to the heavy." Gemini round 45 (`gemini_walk_review/round_45/gemini_original_preview.jpg`; idle, Medium sheet and the Ekko 5H reference attached; the first two sends stalled on a page screenshot timeout until the tab was refreshed): 10 poses - idle, wind-up (Key raised over the shoulder), wind-up peak (Key low and level behind), spin begins, mid-spin with his back to the viewer (blue swirl), coming around (swirl), impact lunge with the Key level out front (big crescent smear), follow-through over the shoulder (dust), heavy settle with the Key pointing down, idle. Its smears are blue crescents like ours. Shown to Kvon.

**Direction, October 27:** Kvon: "I like the poses and suggested adjustments. Since gemini uses the 3d model as a reference, I expect your generated poses to look as close to the generated images as possible." Build `atk_sheavy_vN` to match round-45 poses 2-9 closely, with extra spin in-betweens, arms visible in front (Medium lessons), our solid crescent smears instead of the swirls, no dust effect for now. Feet pivot with the body through the spin (no twisted legs). Frame data: Ekko's heavy 13 startup / 3 active / 21 recovery. (Note: Gemini only sees the attached 2D sprites, not the 3D rig.)

**Redesign, October 27:** After reviewing the spinning-slash build (atk_sheavy_v3), Kvon: "Instead of this animation. I want sora to do an advancing downward slash, stepping forward with his back foot to increase his range." The spin is dropped; Gemini concept art first for the new move (round 46).
