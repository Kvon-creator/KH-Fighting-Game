# Stage: Dark Depths (DBFZ-style layers)

**Fact or rule:** Kvon (November 17): "add a stage using a similar method as DBFZ ... a less detailed background, a more detailed middle ground, and the most detailed foreground where the characters can actually stand and move around on. Use the assets from the stages/ folder." Kvon added `stages/assets/dark depths/` (main.obj ~10k faces, skybox.obj, gearplane.obj + textures).
- **Built in Blender** (`stages/dark_depths_layers.blend`): OBJ import (Y-up, the importer turns it upright); textures made unlit (emission) with their alpha. **gearplane.obj left out** - a big orange gear lying over the platform, looks like a map / save marker, not scenery (it imported standing upright; its own data is flat). Game camera: level, 50 mm lens, 1042 units back, 140 units up, 750 units across at the fighters' line (about five Sora heights, like the game) - the fighters' floor point lands on the game's FLOOR_Y 600. **Raised geometry between the camera and the fighters' line was cut away** (707 faces: a rock ridge at the platform's front edge filled the view), as DBFZ stages do.
- **Layers by distance from the camera** (visible scenery sits in three bands): foreground < 40,000 units (the seabed floor; only 0-5,000 is visible), middle ground 40,000-50,000 (the castle-like buildings, spires, rocks), background >= 50,000 (giant pillars, far terrain) + the sky. Rendered (Eevee) separately at 1280 x 1080: the bottom 720 rows are the game's screen, 360 extra rows above for when the camera rises. Detail: background = a 40% copy blown back up, blurred, 28% haze toward the sky colour, less saturated; middle = full detail, 12% haze; foreground = untouched. Files: `stages/dark_depths_render/` (raw_* = straight renders, bg/mid/fg = finished) -> `KingdomArena/assets/stages/dark_depths/`.
- **Godot:** `scenes/stage_dark_depths.tscn` - three Parallax2D layers (Godot 4.3+, recommended over ParallaxBackground; docs checked): scroll_scale y 0.25 / 0.55 / 1.0, scroll_offset (0, -360); plus the same invisible floor / wall / ceiling bodies and spawn markers as arena.tscn. `match.tscn` now instances it (node "Stage") instead of arena.tscn; **arena.tscn is kept** (switch back by changing that one ext_resource). The camera only moves up and down, so the parallax is vertical only.
- Look: at rest the castle buildings sit mostly below the floor's far edge (they stand lower than the platform) - they rise into view when the camera goes up (jumps, the air grab). No stage select exists.

**Why:** Kvon's request; DBFZ was the reference given.
