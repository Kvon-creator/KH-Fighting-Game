# Stage: Dark Depths (DBFZ-style layers)

**Fact or rule:** Kvon (November 17): "add a stage using a similar method as DBFZ ... a less detailed background, a more detailed middle ground, and the most detailed foreground where the characters can actually stand and move around on. Use the assets from the stages/ folder." Kvon added `stages/assets/dark depths/` (main.obj ~10k faces, skybox.obj, gearplane.obj + textures).
- **Built in Blender** (`stages/dark_depths_layers.blend`): OBJ import (Y-up, the importer turns it upright); textures made unlit (emission) with their alpha. **gearplane.obj left out** - a big orange gear lying over the platform, looks like a map / save marker, not scenery (it imported standing upright; its own data is flat). Game camera: level, 50 mm lens, 1042 units back, 140 units up, 750 units across at the fighters' line (about five Sora heights, like the game) - the fighters' floor point lands on the game's FLOOR_Y 600. **Raised geometry between the camera and the fighters' line was cut away** (707 faces: a rock ridge at the platform's front edge filled the view), as DBFZ stages do.
- **Layers by distance from the camera** (visible scenery sits in three bands): foreground < 40,000 units (the seabed floor; only 0-5,000 is visible), middle ground 40,000-50,000 (the castle-like buildings, spires, rocks), background >= 50,000 (giant pillars, far terrain) + the sky. Rendered (Eevee) separately at 1280 x 1080: the bottom 720 rows are the game's screen, 360 extra rows above for when the camera rises. Detail: background = a 40% copy blown back up, blurred, 28% haze toward the sky colour, less saturated; middle = full detail, 12% haze; foreground = untouched. Files: `stages/dark_depths_render/` (raw_* = straight renders, bg/mid/fg = finished) -> `KingdomArena/assets/stages/dark_depths/`.
- **Godot:** `scenes/stage_dark_depths.tscn` - three Parallax2D layers (Godot 4.3+, recommended over ParallaxBackground; docs checked): scroll_scale y 0.25 / 0.55 / 1.0, scroll_offset (0, -360); plus the same invisible floor / wall / ceiling bodies and spawn markers as arena.tscn. `match.tscn` now instances it (node "Stage") instead of arena.tscn; **arena.tscn is kept** (switch back by changing that one ext_resource). The camera only moves up and down, so the parallax is vertical only.
- Look: at rest the castle buildings sit mostly below the floor's far edge (they stand lower than the platform) - they rise into view when the camera goes up (jumps, the air grab). No stage select exists.

**Why:** Kvon's request; DBFZ was the reference given.

**Castle more prominent (Kvon, November 17, with a reference screenshot from high above the platform looking down at the castle island):** one camera can't give both that view and a natural floor, so the layers now use two cameras (the DBFZ way - each layer is its own picture): the foreground (the platform floor, now < 5,000 units) still from the low game camera; the middle ground (5,000-50,000: the castle island with its ruins and ground, the blue cliff ring) and the background (>= 50,000: pillars, mountains, sky) from a **vista camera** (22 mm lens, 6,000 units up above the platform, pitched 11 deg down at the castle, shift_y 24/1280 so the castle's base sits just above the floor's far edge at game row ~430). The shadow decals under the buildings (material015, texture015: solid black, opaque - its transparency was vertex alpha the OBJ dropped) render at 45% opacity. At rest the pillars' tops are above the screen (they show as the camera rises) - a smaller castle would let them fit. Known flaw: at the camera's highest point (air grab overhead hold) a few flat grey patches of far ground show through gaps on the right of the island layer.

**Side-to-side scrolling + second reference (Kvon, November 17: a Smash Ultimate screenshot of this stage - "should also be a good reference" - and "I also want the map to move side to side as the players move just like when they are jumping, allowing the map to exist beyond the confines of the playable window"):**
- Stage 2560 px wide (two screens - **my choice**, match.gd STAGE_WIDTH). The camera follows the fighters' midpoint sideways, clamped to the stage; the walls are `Fighter.left_limit` / `right_limit` (static vars, were LEFT_LIMIT / RIGHT_LIMIT constants), set every frame by match.gd `_follow_sideways()` to WALL_MARGIN 120 px inside the screen's edges - so Agent Setup's "screen edges are the invisible walls" still holds, the fighters can be at most 1040 px apart (as before), and the stage's ends make corners. START_X moved +640 (1060 / 1500) so every distance-based behaviour is unchanged (grab distances 182.1 / 133.2 identical in tests).
- Parallax2D now scrolls sideways too: scroll_scale (0.25, 0.25) / (0.55, 0.55) / (1, 1); images 1600 / 1984 / 2560 wide (1280 x (1 + scale)) x 1080, rendered wide by widening each camera's sensor with the image (same px per unit). Checked at the left end, centre and right end: no gaps.
- Toward the Smash look: the castle layer lowered 150 px (vista shift_y 174/1280) so its base tucks behind the floor's edge; the background from its own **bg_cam** (18 -> 26 mm, aimed level, shift so the pillar tops sit at the top of the screen and the horizon hides behind the island) - the pillars now dominate the sky with the Heartless-emblem one in the middle; raised geometry removed from the floor layer (a huge cliff wall at the right end and a slope at the left). The Smash version's floating-block platform edge was not copied (the low game camera can't see the platform's front edge).

**Sharper background, floor lower on screen (Kvon, November 17: "the background should be higher quality and lower the interactable foreground lower on the screen to show more detail in the background and middleground"):** the background's deliberate degrading (40% shrink + blur + 28% haze) is gone - now only 12% haze, saturation 0.95; the middle ground 6% haze. All layers render at double size and are scaled down (cleaner edges), Eevee 32 samples. The camera rests 60 px higher (match.gd CAMERA_REST_Y 300, my choice of amount): the fighters' floor (FLOOR_Y 600) shows 660 px down; by parallax the castle drops only 33 px and the pillars 15 px, so more of both shows (screen row = image row - 360 + 60 x scroll scale). The castle re-framed higher (vista shift_y 91/1280) so its buildings sit just above the floor's edge (now ~507 px down). Grab distances unchanged in tests.
